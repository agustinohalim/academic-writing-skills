## Elsevier Researcher Academy — modules by skill stage

Free e-learning at https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com (registration for full access). Most
modules are recorded webinars without transcripts, so only titles and stated objectives were
checked; nothing in this plugin quotes their content. Paths below are relative to that site.

**Research preparation → Research design** (`/research-preparation/research-design`) — before
the article plan (`scientific-article-writing` stage 1):

- How to identify research gaps (12 min)
- How to locate key publications (15 min)
- How to find relevant and authoritative research (15 min)
- Using Generative and Agentic AI in the Literature Review (53 min)
- Gen AI use in the research workflow (53 min)
- Ethical AI in research: Evaluating tools and applying best practices (44 min)

**Research preparation → Research data management** — data availability and code release:
Creating a good research data management plan; How to manage and publish your research data;
Data repositories to store your data; the four *It's Not FAIR!* modules (findable, accessible,
interoperable, reusable).

**Writing for research → Fundamentals of manuscript preparation**
(`/writing-research/fundamentals-manuscript-preparation`) — stages 3–5:

- Structuring your article correctly (40 min)
- How to prepare your manuscript (40 min)
- How to write an abstract and improve your article (40 min)
- Using Generative AI in Manuscript Preparation (15 min)
- Data visualisation series (why and how; choosing the right plot; three contexts)
- Creating visual abstracts, parts 1–4

**Writing for research → Writing skills** (`/writing-research/writing-skills`):

- 10 tips for writing a truly terrible journal article (1 h)
- Using proper manuscript language (40 min, interactive) — pairs with `manuscript-proofreading`
- AI for academic writing: From responsible use to practical application (1 h)
- How to turn your thesis into an article (1 h) — mind the rules on thesis-derived papers
- Writing a persuasive cover letter for your manuscript (8 min) — stage 7
- 5 Diseases ailing research – and how to cure them (45 min)

**Publication process → Finding the right journal**
(`/publication-process/finding-right-journal`) — this skill:

- How to identify suitable journals for submission (15 min; Scopus citation overview, CiteScore, compare sources)
- Using the most relevant metrics to help you choose the right journal (20 min)
- How to easily spot reliable journals (15 min; presenter chaired Scopus CSAB subject areas)
- Trusted publishing vs. predatory journals (1 h)
- Identifying fake acceptance letters (4 min)
- Using AI to match your abstract to the right journal (10 min)
- Understanding manuscript rejection and reasons why (7 min)
- Rejected manuscripts: Next steps and finding the right fit (56 min)
- Transferring your paper and the benefits explained (3 min)
- Appealing the rejection and why you should think twice (2 min)
- Improving your chances of acceptance next time (7 min)

**Navigating peer review → Going through peer review** — revision stage, responses to reviewers.

**Publication process → Ethics** — e.g. Authorship: Understanding author roles and
responsibilities (18 min); pairs with `research-integrity`.
